How to play

1
Draw a nation and season
Each draw lands on one of the six nations from a specific year — Ireland 2018, France 2022, Scotland 1995 and so on.
2
Pick a player
Browse that nation's full squad for the season and choose one player to add to your XV. Sort by rating or position.
3
Build your XV
Keep drawing until all 15 positions are filled. Mix legends from different nations and eras to build the ultimate squad.
4
Enter the Championship
Simulate a full Six Nations campaign. Can your All-Stars win every match and claim the Grand Slam?
